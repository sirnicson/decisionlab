# Scientific basis — V1.2

The environmental cases are research-informed, not scientific simulations. Primary research is used to anchor the **direction of relationships, plausible environmental sensitivities, microbial functions and trade-offs**. Learner-facing deterministic values are synthetic training data engineered for decision quality, constraints, opportunity cost and portfolio reasoning.

The canonical source catalogue is implemented in `src/data/research/researchDeck.ts`. The complete source-to-case traceability and Appendix A references are retained in `docs/UIUX_DATA_SHEET_V1_2_2.md`.

## Data governance

- Red Rock numeric case values are synthetic unless explicitly stated otherwise.
- Sea Wolf Permeability, Mobility and Energy are abstract 1–10 game indices, not laboratory measurements.
- Sea Wolf uses exactly four simplified trait labels: Heat Resistant, Aerobic, Phosphorus Removal and Light Sensitive.
- A trait being desired or undesired is site-specific and is not a universal scientific judgement.
- Scientific sources support the relationship being mirrored; they do not validate the exact game number or score.

## Characteristic-profiled pool correction

The additional profiling-candidate cards introduced in the V1.2.1 patch are synthetic game data. Their values are engineered only to operationalise the locked characteristic-to-pool mechanic. They do not represent laboratory measurements or values copied from the cited microbiology literature. The same research-informed relationship boundary described above continues to apply.
