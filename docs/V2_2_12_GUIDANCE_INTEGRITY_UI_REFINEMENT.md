# V2.2.12 — Guidance integrity and Norvale Exhibit 2 UI refinement

## Sea Wolf guidance rule
Dr. Paula Reyes may explain the decision objective, available information, constraints and trade-offs, but must not identify a preferred option, hidden decision rule, required residual range or scoring formula before the learner makes the relevant decision.

Implemented across Cinder Bay, Tidal Reach and Azure Shelf:
- Site Brief wording is neutral about current-vs-next prioritisation.
- Characteristics guidance teaches selective filtering without naming the preferred attribute/trait.
- Categorisation guidance asks for the most defensible destination without implying a Current → Next → Return hierarchy.
- Learning feedback for correct routing is qualitative and does not expose the 2-of-4/3-of-4 hidden rule.
- Initial Pool feedback discusses coverage/gaps without revealing the preferred profiling formula.
- Prospect Selection guidance remains portfolio-oriented and unchanged in principle.
- Two-slot Treatment guidance no longer calculates the exact P/M/E residual range for the third microbe.
- Treatment Result no longer states the 20-percentage-point deduction formula.

No Sea Wolf numeric data, fixed decks, route keys, prospect decisions, treatment-effectiveness criteria, feasible maxima or scoring weights are changed.
