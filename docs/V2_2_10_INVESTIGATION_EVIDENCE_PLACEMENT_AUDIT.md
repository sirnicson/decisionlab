# V2.2.10 — Red Rock Investigation Evidence Placement Audit

This update corrects three Investigation Exhibit 2 issues found during the evidence-flow audit without changing source values, answer keys, report fields, scoring or task order.

## Salvanova Forest
- The no-intervention Year-5 vegetation assumption (65%) now sits in Study Information as client-supplied context.
- Exhibit 2 now shows only the wolf-reintroduction vegetation and tourism projections.
- The former no-wolf comparison row/reference mark is removed.
- Analysis Question 4 now directs the learner back to Study Information for the 65% input.

## Caldera Marsh
- Exhibit 2 now contains programme thresholds only.
- The former `First year met` column and threshold-timing bars are removed.
- The visual overlays each threshold on its Exhibit 1 projection without labelling the first year achieved.
- The learner must determine the first year the full threshold set is met during Analysis.

## Norvale Highlands
- Exhibit 2 retains the raw programme-input table.
- The former calculated planting-runway and programme-cost-vs-budget views are removed.
- The visual now compares only raw same-unit programme scale: wildfire-affected area versus planned active restoration.
- Programme cost, budget headroom and planting duration remain learner calculations in Analysis.

## Global rule
Investigation exhibits may display observed data, supplied projections, programme criteria and raw client assumptions. They must not display a calculated conclusion that a later Analysis question explicitly asks the learner to derive.
