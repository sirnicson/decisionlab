## V2.4.1 correction

Exhibit 1 chart values for Years 0–4 are journalised from chart-cell context and therefore use the compact series/year pattern (`No wolves · Year N`, `With wolves · Year N`). Year 5 uses explicit evidence bindings to named scenario facts, whose labels previously used a different prose form.

The two Year 5 fact labels are now:

- `Exhibit 1 — No Wolves — Year 5`
- `Exhibit 1 — With Wolves — Year 5`

The existing journal compact-label formatter converts these to:

- `No wolves · Year 5`
- `With wolves · Year 5`

## Locked V2.3.10 interaction baseline

V2.3.10 Research Journal behaviour remains intact, including pointer-driven vertical reordering, full-height reorder grip, live insertion placeholder, edge auto-scroll, keyboard Up/Down reordering, evidence drag to active work fields, circular Important/Unmark and Return controls, and `RESEARCH JOURNAL · COLLECTED EVIDENCE` heading.
