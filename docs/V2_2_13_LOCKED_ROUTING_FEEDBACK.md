# V2.2.13 — Locked-routing feedback alignment

## Change
Sea Wolf Learning Mode routing feedback now respects the fact that Categorisation and Transfer Review allocations are committed before feedback is shown.

- Correct route: neutral reinforcement without revealing a hidden scoring rule.
- Non-reference route: `This may not be the strongest route...` with guidance for remaining site decisions.
- No feedback tells the learner to recheck, redo or change the committed card.
- Final-site copy automatically omits next-site information.

## Preservation
No Sea Wolf numeric data, route keys, decks, site requirements, transfer mechanics, prospect logic, treatment criteria, feasible maxima or scoring weights changed.
