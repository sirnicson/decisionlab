# A note about this project

DecisionLab grew from my interest in how people investigate evidence, work through uncertainty, and explain a decision—not just whether they reach the expected answer.

The experience is set inside a fictional environmental advisory engagement. Its cases, organisations, characters, and learner-facing values are synthetic. Published scientific research helps ground the relationships and trade-offs, but DecisionLab is an educational game rather than a scientific simulator.
