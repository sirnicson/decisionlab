# A note about this project

DecisionLab grew from my interest in how people investigate evidence, work through uncertainty, and explain a decision—not just whether they reach the expected answer.

The experience is set inside a fictional environmental advisory engagement. Its cases, organisations, characters, and learner-facing values are synthetic. Published scientific research helps ground the relationships and trade-offs, but DecisionLab is an educational game rather than a scientific simulator.

This is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by McKinsey & Company. It does not claim to reproduce an official assessment, scoring model, percentile, pass threshold, or hiring outcome.

Contributions and playful experimentation are welcome. Please build on the original work rather than adding proprietary logos, protected screenshots, copied assessment questions, or claims of official equivalence. New ideas should remain original, clearly sourced where appropriate, and respectful of the project's educational purpose.
